# Code of Conduct

Novix UI follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include using welcoming language, respecting differing viewpoints, gracefully accepting constructive criticism, and focusing on what is best for the community.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to conduct@novix-ui.dev. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
